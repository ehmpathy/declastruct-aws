### the care grades, with BOTH terms of the product

### 🔴 the cell-12 lesson, generalized

the note below records cell 12's regrade and drew this lesson: *"when a cell's fallback is 'the
console', check whether the tool converges without it."* **that is narrower than the law that
produced it** — it patches the one phrase that burned us.

⇒ **the law: a fallback assertion with no cost term is half a grade.** *some* fallback always
exists (the console, a re-apply, a human with admin creds), so the question is never *"is there
one?"* but *"is `freq × cost` tolerable?"* — and a mis-named alter that hides intolerable severity
is graded a **blocker** by the care brief, not a nitpick.

⚠️ 🔴 **cell 12 was in this list and has been removed from it.** it was graded alter on the strength
of *"fallback = console"*, and that phrase hid a permadrift: with no `delPublicAccessBlock`, the
retract writes no change and the plan reads UPDATE forever. **a fallback that requires a human to
edit the cloud by hand before the tool converges is not a fallback — it is the failure the wish
exists to prevent.** the lesson generalizes: when a cell's fallback is *"the console"*, check
whether the tool converges without it. if it does not, the cell is a critipath wearing an
alterpath's grade.
