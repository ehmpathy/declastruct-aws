# wish: a demo-account role the ahbode camp grove can assume

## .what

a **dedicated** iam role in the **ehmpathy demo** aws account that:

1. **trusts** the ahbode camp grove role as a principal, and
2. carries **everyday-power** permission in demo — enough for routine work, not enough to mint or
   rewrite identity

after this lands, this succeeds **from an ahbode grove box**, with the ambient instance-role
credential it already holds:

```sh
aws sts assume-role --role-arn arn:aws:iam::<demo-account-id>:role/<the-new-role> \
  --role-session-name grove
```

and the returned credential can do everyday demo work.

## .why

ahbode groves are the boxes that do the work. this repo's own integration and acceptance suites
run against the demo account, and today a clone on a grove has **no path to it** — so demo work
either gets a credential handed to it out of band, or it moves off the grove entirely.

⇒ the grant belongs here because **the account belongs here**. `provision/aws.auth/account=demo/`
is where this repo declares who may act in demo, and a cross-account trust is exactly that
declaration.

## 🔴 .this is the TARGET half. the CALLER half is in ANOTHER ORG

the paired tree is `ahbode/infrastructure`, which adds a third `sts:AssumeRole` statement to the
camp grove role's `grove-reach` inline policy. its wish is
`2026-09-06.dispatch.repo=infrastructure.behavior=feat-grove-reach-ehmpathy-demo.wish.md`.

that repo states the invariant you inherit, verbatim
(`provision/aws.infra/account=camp/resources.reach.ts:28-31`):

> *"a reach is wired only once the target trust in provision/aws.auth has ALSO landed, and a
> half-wired one fails at runtime with **AccessDenied, never at apply time** — so verify both
> halves live."*

⚠️ **so a green apply here proves naught about the reach.** until the ahbode side merges, the
assume-role call fails — and it fails at *runtime*, invisible to an apply. plan for that.

## .what is already true in-repo — treat as ground, re-verify if time has passed

**1. `provision/aws.auth/account=demo/` is the paved home** for demo-account identity. it already
declares `ehmpathy-demo-oidc` — a role trusted via github's oidc provider, for cicd.

**2. `demoPermissionsPolicy` is the single source of truth for demo power.**
`provision/aws.auth/resources.common.ts:37` declares it, and its own `.note` says it is *"reused
by both SSO permission set and OIDC role."* so it already has **two** consumers:

- the SSO permission set, in `account=.root/resources.demo.sso.ts`
- the OIDC role, in `account=demo/resources.oidc.ts`

**3. 🔴 a change to that shared bundle must re-apply BOTH consumers.** the file records this as a
named hazard, at `resources.common.ts:252-253`:

> *"note: a change here must re-apply BOTH the SSO permission set (account=.root) AND the OIDC
> role (account=demo) per hazard.local-green-cicd-red.oidc-role-not-reapplied"*

⚠️ **a third consumer gives that hazard a third arm** — and its name says exactly how it bites:
local green, cicd red. read that hazard brief before you decide to reuse.

**4. that bundle is WIDE, and it crosses the `iam:` line.** `demoPermissionsPolicy` grants
`iam:CreateRole`, `iam:UpdateAssumeRolePolicy`, `iam:PutRolePolicy`, `iam:PassRole`,
`iam:CreateAccessKey`, and more — plus `budgets:*`, `ce:*`, `ses:*`.

🔴 **that matters because of what the caller-side vocabulary says.** ahbode's `term=tier` declares
the ladder `everyday-reader < everyday-power < emergency-admin`, and states that the admin tier is
*"reserved for the acts that mint or rewrite identity itself (every `iam:` write)."*

⇒ so **"everyday power" and `demoPermissionsPolicy` are not obviously the same set.** reuse the
bundle as-is and you hand a grove more than the word "everyday power" promises. that is the
central fulcrum below — and it may be entirely fine, since demo is demo. it is a decision to make
out loud, never one to inherit by convenience.

## 🔴 .the constraint that will tempt you most — a reach OWNS its target

`ehmpathy-demo-oidc` already exists, and a second trust statement bolted onto it would be the
fastest possible path. **the caller-side repo forbids exactly this** (`resources.reach.ts:24-26`):

> *"each target is a role DEDICATED to this reach […] never one shared with cicd — so a widen for
> one consumer can never silently widen the other"*

⇒ a role shared between github-actions cicd and a grove means **any future widen for either one
silently widens the other**, and a reviewer of that future change sees only one consumer. the role
must be **new and dedicated.**

⚠️ this rule lives in the *other* repo, so it will not appear in your reviewers. it is stated here
because it binds you anyway — it is a property of the grant, not of the repo that holds it.

## .the fulcrums — yours to settle, itemized

**1. the permission bound.** the wisher said *"everyday power"* and did not enumerate actions.

| path | what it costs |
|---|---|
| reuse `demoPermissionsPolicy` | fast, one source of truth, consistent with cicd. but it grants the grove `iam:` writes, which the caller-side tier ladder reserves for `emergency-admin`. and it gives the re-apply hazard a third arm |
| declare a grove-specific bundle | honest to the tier word, and a grove is the most exposed box in the fleet. but a second bundle is a second artifact to drift |

⚠️ **this fulcrum is SHARED with the paired ahbode tree.** whatever you decide, say it loudly
enough that the other half can contradict you — and read their yield if it landed first.

**2. what to call the role.** not decided. it should read as *whose reach it is* and *at what
tier*. `ahbode-prep-for-grove` / `ahbode-prod-for-grove` are the caller side's convention for the
same concept, if a mirror helps.

**3. whether the demo account id belongs in code here.** the ahbode side hardcodes account ids on
purpose, with a stated rationale. this repo reads its account id from live credentials
(`resources.oidc.ts:24`). those are two conventions that meet at one grant. no view on which wins
— just do not let the value end up in two places that can disagree.
