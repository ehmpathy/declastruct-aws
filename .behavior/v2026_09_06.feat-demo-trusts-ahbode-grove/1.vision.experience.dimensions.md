# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

## .what

the orthogonal axes the experience of *"a demo-account role the ahbode camp grove can assume"*
varies over. the product of these axes is the full experience space. the inventory
(`1.vision.experience.case=_.md`) walks every cell; the demos
(`1.vision.experience.case=N.$slug.md`) render the critipaths.

⇒ this is `howto.experience.decompose` applied. the critipaths fall out of the grid, never out of
memory.

### 🔴 the OTHER latent dimensions — folded on purpose, recorded so the fold is auditable

⚠️ **F1 is not the only candidate axis kept out of the box.** each row below was folded for a
different reason, and every one was **surfaced in prose and invisible from the grid** until it was
verdicted here. ⇒ a reviewer of the *discovery* must be able to **verify** a fold rather than infer
it, so each is verdicted below beside its reason.

| candidate axis | values | the verdict | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **duration / credential-delivery** | short run vs run ≥ session life · static env-var session vs auto-refresh ini profile | **folded into per-cell grades** (`case=1`'s header) | see below |
| 🔴 **the `declare` sense** | create · converge · revoke | **mapped onto axis C, never crossed with it** | see below |
| 🔴 **vault reachability** (a resource state) | the grove reaches the keyrack vault · it does not | **folded as a conditional residual** (`case=1` `[t4]`, the edgecases table) | see below |
| 🔴 **collaborator identity** | the ahbode camp grove · any further third-party collaborator | **folded as a REPLICA of the whole grid** | see below |

#### collaborator identity — a fold that is a CONSEQUENCE of a pick, never a fact about the domain

the wisher widened the scope to *"any third party collaborator account."* under **F11's pick — one
dedicated role per collaborator** — a second collaborator is a **replica** of this entire grid: same
four actors, same three stages, same four reach-states, and **not one cell's verdict moves**. an axis
that multiplies the product and changes no verdict is orthogonal and non-informative, which is
precisely a fold.

🔴 **and the fold is EARNED by the pick, so it is not durable on its own.** under the alternative —
one shared role that holds N principals — it would **not** fold: a revoke in one collaborator's row
changes another's cell, so the axis would carry information and the product would have to be walked.

⇒ **the re-walk trigger is a collaborator whose trust SHAPE differs**, never merely a second name: a
`Condition` (F9), a different bundle (F1), or a different tier. a plain copy needs no walk; a
divergent one re-opens the whole product.

⚠️ **this is the one fold on the list a driver could not have found by a harder look at the artifact.**
the other three were latent in the design; this one arrived from the wisher, after every cell above
had been verdicted against a single consumer.

#### vault reachability — the resource state the rule names among the usual omissions

`howto.experience.decompose` names **resource-absent states** among the dimensions authors drop, and
this one is real: the wish's central critipath cell — `grove-clone × act × both` — splits on whether
the box can reach the keyrack vault.

| the grove | what happens |
|---|---|
| **cannot** reach the vault (**the assumed case**) | `sourceAllKeysIntoEnv.js:192-194` injects only `granted` keys, so **no `AWS_PROFILE` is set**. the sdk's first link runs `fromEnv` and **the exported session wins** ⇒ the happy path |
| 🔴 **can** reach the vault | `keyrack.source` sets `AWS_PROFILE` → the sdk **bypasses** the session (`defaultProvider.js:7-33`) → `fromIni` finds no such profile → the chain falls to **IMDS** → the suite runs as **the box's camp badge** and gets a **third byte-identical `AccessDenied`** (question 16(b)) |

**the fold, and its reason:** the second row is a **conditional** state, never a second experience.
it is barred today by a fact about the grove fleet — no camp grove holds an ehmpathy vault — so a
cross would generate a column that is `impossible` in every cell **under the state we assume**, and
would flip wholesale the day that fact changes. ⇒ it is the same shape as F1: a **latent** axis
whose values are decided by one external condition, so it belongs where a condition belongs and not
dissolved into cells that would read as settled.

⚠️ **and the residual is walked, not merely noted** — `case=1` `[t4]` carries it as an explicit
conditional, and the edgecases table gives it its own row precisely because its `AccessDenied` is
indistinguishable from two others. **the path is walked; only the axis is unnamed.**

🔴 **the bar is NURTURE, so it owes a checkable invariant** — the same treatment the `cicd × declare`
cells get in `case=_`, and for the same reason: a fleet fact is a **choice**, and a choice a reader
must reconstruct from prose is one a future change can break with no stated rule to violate.

> **no camp grove box holds a reachable ehmpathy keyrack vault.** the happy path in `case=1` `[t4]`
> depends on it: with no `granted` key, `keyrack.source` sets no `AWS_PROFILE`, so the sdk's `fromEnv`
> link runs and the exported session wins. **a fleet change that grants a grove box an ehmpathy vault
> violates this invariant** and owes a re-walk of this axis plus a re-grade of `case=1` `[t4]`.

⚠️ **and it is not merely a doc obligation — it is a one-command check.** `rhx keyrack status --owner
ehmpath` on a grove box settles it, which is what parts a checkable invariant from an assumption.

⇒ **the trigger: should any grove box gain vault reach, this becomes a real axis** — because then
both values are live at once, and the two produce different experiences from one cell.

🔴 **and it is the sharpest of the three folds, for a reason the other two do not share.** duration
and the `declare` sense were both **visible as dimensions** and folded deliberately. this one hides
as a **fault-diagnosis note** — an ambiguous error message. ⇒ **an error text that three distinct
states produce is the classic tell of an absent axis**, and it is the same signal that grew axis C
from three values to four. **twice over, the tell is two states that are indistinguishable from the
outside.**

#### duration — the dimension the rule warns is the usual omission

`howto.experience.decompose` names **concurrency / time** as *"the dimension that surfaces the
non-narratable critipaths"*, and it is exactly right here: the sharpest edge in this vision is a
suite run that outlives its 1h session and dies mid-flight (`case=1` `[t6]`, `case=5` `[t4]`,
`case=7` `[t4']`). ⇒ **the omission is real and the fold is still the right call — for exactly ONE
reason, and the count is deliberate:**

🔴 **the delivery shape is a property we intend to CHANGE, never a stable one.** question 17
prescribes an ini profile with `credential_source = Ec2InstanceMetadata`, which auto-refreshes and
**removes the 1h bound entirely**. ⇒ an axis minted on today's delivery shape would encode a defect
we plan to fix.

⛔ **and there is a SECOND reason available here that must NOT be reached for**, because it is
unsound and it inverts the rule it invokes: *"duration is not orthogonal to axis B — it has no sense
at `obtain`, so a cross would generate a column of inapplicable cells."*

| the claim | why it fails |
|---|---|
| *"a value has no sense at one stage ⇒ not orthogonal"* | orthogonality is about whether a value on one axis **implies** a value on another. an inapplicable cell is a **cell verdict** — `impossible` — which this product already carries four of. a cell you can verdict is a cell the axes generated **correctly** |
| *"inapplicable cells are not a walk"* | 🔴 **they are exactly a walk.** `howto.experience.decompose`'s completeness-by-construction comes from a verdict on **every** cell, `impossible` included. a column of them is a walk that found a bound |

⇒ **the general form: "this value does not apply there" argues FOR an axis, never against one** —
it is the axis that lets you state the bound as a checkable cell rather than leave it in prose. an
argument of that shape, used to justify a fold, would fold every axis that has any barred cell.

⚠️ **and the reason to name it rather than omit it: a fold justified by two reasons where one is
wrong is WEAKER than a fold justified by one**, because the wrong one is what a reviewer tests. ⇒
the delivery shape carries this fold alone, and it carries it fully.

⚠️ **and the fold is graded per cell, so no path is lost** — `case=1`'s header splits `act × both`
into a happy short run and a **sharp** overrun, and `case=_`'s walked-product row carries the same
split. **the paths are walked; only the axis is unnamed.**

⇒ **the trigger: should question 17 measure an overrun AND the ini fix be declined, duration becomes
a real axis** — because then the bound is permanent rather than transitional.

#### the `declare` sense — mapped onto axis C because the two are NOT independent

`declare` ranges over three end states, and the product does not cross them with axis C. **that is
deliberate, and the reason is an invariant rather than a convenience:**

| sense | the reach-state it can occur in | why the others are barred |
|---|---|---|
| **create** | `neither` | you cannot create a role that exists |
| **converge** | `both`, `target-only` | you cannot converge on a role that is absent |
| **revoke** | `both`, `target-only` → `revoked` | you cannot revoke a reach that was never granted |

⚠️ **the `create` row once also named `caller-only`, and that is not a value of this axis** — the
four are `both`, `target-only`, `neither`, `revoked`. the state it reached for is real (their half
merged first, ours still absent) and **axis C folds it into `neither`**: our half is absent, the
reach denies, and our create is identical either way. ⇒ **from this repo's actors the two are
indistinguishable**, which is what earns the fold. 🔴 **the defect it illustrates is narrower than a
typo**: a table written when the axis held a different value-set kept the old name after the axis was
corrected, so **an axis rename must sweep its DEPENDENT tables**, never the axis row alone.

⇒ **sense and reach-state are coupled by definition**, so a full 3 × 4 cross would generate **twelve
cells of which five are forbidden by that invariant** — a wider box that carries less information
than this map does. ⚠️ **the one genuine sub-cell IS in the product** (`rogue-process × declare ×
both`, create vs revoke), because there the two senses are **both** reachable in one state and
produce different experiences.

⚠️ **the honest residual: a reader of the grid sees one sense per row and cannot see the cross.**
this table is that cross, and it is the repair — the mapping is now checkable from `dimensions.md`
rather than reconstructable from three case files.

⇒ **the trigger is explicit, and it did not fire: F1 upheld the reused bundle 2026-09-08.** it stays
live as a **change** trigger — should a later change narrow the bundle, re-walk: promote the bound to
an axis, or drop the nine cells and restate the census. this note is the instruction that round
inherits.

## the axes

### axis A — actor (4 values)

who lives through the behavior.

| value | who |
|-------|-----|
| `grove-clone` | an agent at work on **any** ahbode camp grove box, under that box's ambient instance role |
| `rogue-process` | any OTHER process on **any** grove box, at any uid |
| `provisioner` | the human who applies `provision/aws.auth/account=demo/resources.ts` with demo admin creds |
| `cicd-pipeline` | github actions, federated onto `ehmpathy-demo-oidc` — the **incumbent** consumer |

🔴 **the grant is FLEET-WIDE and FUTURE-INCLUSIVE, never per-box.** the principal we trust is a
**role** — `<camp-grove-role-name>` — and ahbode's own cloudtrail sample shows the identity form
`<camp-grove-role-name>/<instance-id>` (`provision/aws.infra/account=camp/resources.grove-probe.ts:68`):
**one role, one instance-id per box.** so:

- every camp grove box holds this reach, not the one box someone had in mind
- **a grove box provisioned tomorrow inherits demo access automatically** — no change here, no
  review here, no signal here
- the fleet's size and growth are governed in **another org's repo**

⇒ so `case=5`'s blast radius is not *"every process on the box"*; it is **every process on every camp
grove box, now and future**. that is a materially larger number, and it is the number F1 must be
priced against.

⚠️ **`rogue-process` is a real actor, never a paranoid flourish.** ahbode's `term=camper` records
that the grove box answers IMDS for **every uid**, so the grove's badge belongs to every process on
the box by construction. the credential is not scoped to the clone that "meant" to use it. so a demo
grant to the grove is a demo grant to the whole box, and that must be lived through, never assumed
away.

**two further candidates were weighed and folded in**, recorded so a later reader knows they were
walked rather than overlooked:

| candidate | why it is not an axis value |
|-----------|----------------------------|
| **auditor** — a human who reads cloudtrail post-incident | it performs no `obtain` and no `declare`, so **8 of its 12** cells would read impossible (`obtain × 4` + `declare × 4`); only `act × 4` survives. its whole experience is one `then` about attribution, which lands in `case=5` `[t5]` and `case=7` `[t2]`. ⚠️ **the `8 of 12` is DERIVED from axis C's width** — a sub-count stated as a literal goes stale the moment an axis grows, so state the derivation |
| **ahbode-provisioner** — the human who applies the caller half | every one of its cells sits in **another org's account**, outside this repo's reach. the dependency is real and appears as the unobservable event in `case=1` `[t1]` and `case=7` `[t1]` |

### axis B — stage (3 values)

what the actor does at that moment. these are stages of one journey, so an actor may occupy each of
them — the axis stays orthogonal to actor.

| value | the act |
|-------|---------|
| `obtain` | acquire a demo credential (`sts:AssumeRole`, or an sso/oidc login) |
| `act` | do work in demo under a credential already held |
| `declare` | change what demo grants — apply a wish, or issue an `iam:` write |

🔴 **`declare` ranges over three end states, and all three are owed a walk.** a declarative
provisioner does not merely create; it converges and it revokes, and each lands a different plan
verdict and a different experience:

| sense | the plan reads | demoed by |
|-------|----------------|-----------|
| **create** — the grant does not exist | `CREATE` ×3, incumbents `KEEP` | `case=3` |
| **converge** — it exists and matches | all `KEEP` | `case=6` |
| **revoke** — it exists and should not | 🔴 **`UPDATE` (upsert) only — `DELETE` is not expressible** on the role. `DESTROY` on its attachments IS | `case=7` |

⚠️ **the revoke row names ONE verdict, and the singular is deliberate — a `UPDATE` / `DELETE` slash
here would be the exact trap the rest of this vision warns against.** `DeclaredAwsIamRoleDao` declares
`set.delete = null` and `src/**` holds no `DeleteRoleCommand`, so a delete of the **role** is a
**silent non-revoke** (`case=7` `[t3]`/`[t3']`). the two attachment DAOs *do* implement delete, which
is why the revoke is a `DESTROY` on the attachments and an `UPDATE` on the role — **two applies, in
that order.** ⇒ **a decomposition table is read as a menu**, so a verdict listed there is a gesture a
reader will reach for; to leave a falsified one in place is to invite the trap from the one artifact
the coverage contract is derived from.

🔴 **the sense split is a property of the AXIS, so it applies to EVERY actor that can `declare` — not
to the provisioner alone.** the table above names only the provisioner's three demos, and that
narrow read leaves two cells unwalked:

| cell | the create sense | 🔴 the revoke sense |
|------|-----------------|--------------------|
| `rogue-process × declare × both` | plant a role — `case=5` `[t2]` | **rewrite a trust policy** — `case=5` `[t2']` |
| `grove-clone × declare × both` | same credential, benign intent | same credential, same power |

⚠️ **the propagation carries TWO of the three senses, and the drop of `converge` is owed a reason
rather than a silence.** `converge` is defined one table up by a **plan verdict** — *"it exists and
matches; the plan reads all `KEEP`"* — which presumes a declastruct wish, a stored declaration, and a
`plan` to read. a `rogue-process` and a `grove-clone` reach demo by direct `iam:` calls; they hold no
wish and read no plan. ⇒ `converge` is **inapplicable to the actor**, never absent from the grid, so
it generates no cell and owes no demo. ⚠️ **and this argument ends where it ends, on purpose — it
does NOT close by a cite of the forbidden count.** that count is **4** (`cicd-pipeline × declare ×
×4`, nurture-barred), and an earlier appeal to `0 forbidden` here would have decayed with it while
the `converge` argument stayed sound. ⇒ **an argument that ends by a cite of another claim inherits
that claim's decay** — so end on your own.

⇒ so the axis carries **three** senses and propagates **two**, by a property of the actor rather than
of the axis. stated here because *"a property of the AXIS, so it applies to EVERY actor"* reads as a
promise of all three, and a reader who counts cells from that sentence would expect two more.

⚠️ **the revoke sense is the sharper of the two for a hostile actor, and it is a different attack.**
`create` adds a resource an audit can find; `revoke` **edits one that already exists** — the reach's
own trust policy, or `ehmpathy-demo-oidc`'s. see `case=5` `[t2']` for what that costs, and why it
narrows `case=4`'s isolation claim.

⇒ **the general form: whenever an axis value carries sub-senses, the split ranges over the whole
axis.** to apply it to one actor and stop is to coarsen the product silently — the inventory still
reads "every cell verdicted" while a sense of one cell went unwalked.

### 🔴 …and *"propagates"* holds TWO claims — separate them or the count reads as an omission

the table above marks **both** cells as two-sensed; the product counts **one** sub-cell:

| the claim *"propagates"* holds | true of |
|---|---|
| **the QUESTION applies** — ask both senses of this actor | ✅ **both** actors |
| **a ROW is generated** — the two senses are distinct experiences | ✅ `rogue-process` only |

for `grove-clone` the split was **asked and collapsed**: create and revoke are the *same credential
with the same power*, so they differ in intent and not in experience — and intent is invisible at the
aws layer, which is `case=5`'s own thesis. `rogue-process`'s two senses differ in **artifact**
(a new role vs an edit), **audit signature** (a findable arn vs no new row), and **survival** across
`case=7`. only that pair earns a row.

⇒ **an axis property and a product row are different claims, and one verb held both.** the count
stands at **48 + 1**, and the collapse is a recorded verdict in `case=_` rather than an inference a
reader must make.

⚠️ **the same shape as the `converge` paragraph two above** — a sense that ranges over the axis and
generates no cell for a given actor. **the difference is the reason**: `converge` is inapplicable *by
the actor's nature* (it reads no plan), while `grove-clone`'s revoke sense is applicable and
**collapses onto its peer**. ⇒ two distinct ways a propagated sense yields no row.

### axis C — reach-state (**4** values)

a reach is a **pair** of grants (ahbode `term=reach`). this repo owns one half. so the live world
has four states, and this repo's apply can distinguish none of them.

| value | target half (this repo) | caller half (`ahbode/infrastructure`) | the reach |
|-------|-------------------------|----------------------------------------|---|
| `both` | live, and it **trusts** | live | works |
| `target-only` | live, and it **trusts** | absent | denies |
| `neither` | absent | absent | denies |
| 🔴 `revoked` | **live, and it REFUSES** | **live** | denies |

🔴 **this axis is the wish's central hazard, made a dimension.** the paired repo states it verbatim
(`provision/aws.infra/account=camp/resources.reach.ts:28-31`): a half-wired reach *"fails at runtime
with AccessDenied, never at apply time"*. so `target-only` is a state a **green apply cannot rule
out** — exactly why it earns an axis rather than a footnote.

## 🔴 the fourth value — and why a `presence` read of this axis excludes it wrongly

⛔ **a path in this repo produces it, and it is the one our own runbook prescribes: the REVOKE.**

⇒ that fits **no** original row. not `both` (the reach denies), not `target-only` (the caller half is

🔴 **the error, stated plainly: presence is the wrong predicate for our own half.** the caller half has

⚠️ **and the evidence was the vision's own headline interaction find.** `case=7` `[t4]` and `case=2`

⇒ **the product grows from 36 to 48 cells** (4 × 3 × **4**), plus the `declare` sub-cell ⇒ **49 rows**.

## orthogonality — checked, never assumed

⚠️ **orthogonal means INDEPENDENTLY VARIABLE, never "produces a distinct outcome".** the two are
easy to conflate, and the conflation carries weight here: a pair of axes that collapse on some slice
is still orthogonal — the collapse is a **fact about the world**, not a defect in the grid. the
section below exists precisely to hold those collapses, so the evidence column must not claim a
distinctness it does not need.

| pair | orthogonal? | evidence |
|------|-------------|----------|
| actor × stage | ✅ **yes — and unconditionally, now that F1 is ruled** | a provisioner obtains admin creds, acts under them, and declares. a `grove-clone` and a `rogue-process` can `declare` **because the reused bundle carries `iam:` writes**, and the wisher upheld that bundle — see below |
| actor × reach-state | ✅ yes | the reach-state is a property of the world, never of who looks at it |
| stage × reach-state | ✅ yes | each can be set with no constraint on the other. ⚠️ **not** *"each stage has a distinct outcome per reach-state"* — collapse 2 below is a live counterexample to that stronger claim, and it does not threaten orthogonality |

🔴 **all three rows hold over the FOURTH reach-state as well, and one of them owes the argument
spelled out.**

`revoked` is **produced by** the `declare` stage, which reads at first like a constraint of stage on
reach-state. **it is not.** orthogonality asks whether every pair is *reachable*, never whether one
can cause the other — and every stage is reachable under `revoked`: a clone can `obtain` (and be
refused), `act` (on a token held from before), or `declare` (during the revocation tail). ⇒ **"X
produces Y" and "X constrains Y" are different claims**, and only the second would break an axis.

⚠️ **this re-proof is owed by construction, and it is exactly the step a grown axis invites you to
skip.** an independence table written over 3 values and left standing beside 4 carries a claim
nobody checked.

⚠️ **what survives the verdict is a CHANGE TRIGGER, never an open grade.** should a later change

🔴 **the durable distinction survives the verdict, and it is the reusable part: a decomposition is

## the collapses the walk found — each is a finding

a whole slice that behaves identically across an axis is a signal, per
`howto.experience.decompose`. three showed up, and each names a real property:

1. **`cicd-pipeline` is invariant across axis C.** its path never touches the reach, so all **12** of
   its cells collapse to one experience. ⇒ that invariance **is** the no-regression guarantee this
   wish owes. it earns a demo *because* it collapses, never despite it (`case=4`).
   ⚠️ **and the collapse STRENGTHENS as axis C widens** — each further reach-state cicd is blind to
   is one more cell of evidence for the same guarantee. `revoked` is the fourth.

2. **`provisioner × declare` is invariant across `target-only` vs `both` — in the PLAN alone.** our
   apply reads the demo account only; the caller half lives in another org's account, invisible to
   it. ⇒ the provisioner **cannot** tell a wired reach from a half-wired one. that is no gap in the
   demo — it is the property that makes `case=2` a critipath.

   ⚠️ **the collapse holds for the diff and NOT for the consequence.** a revoke against `both` ends
   a live grant; the same delete against `target-only` ends a dormant one. identical plan, two
   different acts — which is why `case=7` demos the `both` cell rather than lean on `case=6`.

   🔴 **and `revoked` sharpens this collapse rather than widens it.** under `revoked` the plan reads
   the demo account and sees our own refusal — so the provisioner **can** tell `revoked` from
   `both`. what stays invisible is the **caller half**: they cannot tell *"revoked, and their grant
   dropped"* from *"revoked, and their grant still live"* (`case=7` `[t6]`). ⇒ **the invariance is
   not about the reach-state at all; it is about the FOREIGN HALF.** state it that way, and the
   fourth value confirms it rather than complicates it — state it by reach-state and you name the
   symptom.

3. **`rogue-process` and `grove-clone` share axis C behaviour exactly.** they differ at `declare`
   alone. ⇒ the two actors are one credential that wears two intents, and the only boundary between
   them is the **permission bound** — precisely the wish's named fulcrum F1, ruled 2026-09-08 in
   favor of the reused bundle. ⚠️ **so the boundary between the two actors is INTENT alone**, and the
   verdict makes that permanent rather than provisional.

## the latent dimension it surfaced — and why it stays out of the box

the `rogue-process × declare` cell forced a question no free-list would have raised: **can the
credential we grant rewrite the grant itself?** with `demoPermissionsPolicy` reused as-is, it can —
that bundle carries `iam:UpdateAssumeRolePolicy`, `iam:CreateRole`, `iam:AttachRolePolicy`, and
`iam:CreateAccessKey`, each on `resource: '*'`
(`provision/aws.auth/resources.common.ts:67-107`).

so a **permission-bound** axis (`reuse-shared` / `grove-specific`) genuinely varies the outcome of
that one cell. it stays **out** of the product deliberately, carried as fulcrum F1 instead: it was a
*design choice still open*, never a state the world presents. to make it an axis would double the
box to **96** cells to express one decision — and would smuggle that decision in as already settled.

⚠️ **and the count is the weak half of that argument, so do not lean on it.** ⇒ **the reason a
design choice stays out of the product is the SECOND clause, never the first**: an axis asserts the
world presents both values, and F1's did not both exist — one was a proposal. a cell count grows with
every axis, so it is a poor foundation for a fold.

⇒ 🔴 **and the verdict confirms the fold.** the wisher ruled 2026-09-08 that the reused bundle
stands, ⇒ the second value was never a state of the world and now never will be. `case=5`
demonstrates that cell under the ruled design, and its record of what a different bundle would change
survives as a change trigger.
