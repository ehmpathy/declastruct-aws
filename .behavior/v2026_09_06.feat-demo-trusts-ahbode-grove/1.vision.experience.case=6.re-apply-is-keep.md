## the narrative

it is a week after the grant landed, and the provisioner is not here for this role at all — they are
here to add a budget alarm. the demo provision is one wish, so the plan sweeps the role too.

they run the plan and read the diff top to bottom. `ehmpathy-demo-for-grove` — `KEEP`. its inline
attachment — `KEEP`. its managed attachments — `KEEP`. **two minutes, and the reach is not their
problem today.** they scroll past it to the rows they came for.

⚠️ **that scroll-past is the experience, and it is the whole value.** a provisioner who trusts a
`KEEP` never audits the reach by hand — not on this visit, nor on any of the dozens after it. the
grant becomes furniture: present, unremarkable, and not re-litigated each time someone walks past.

🔴 **and it is exactly what a single spurious `UPDATE` would take away.** the provisioner who sees an
`UPDATE` on this role has to stop and decide: *did someone edit it? did aws? is the reach broken?*
they open the console, compare by hand, find no real change — and next week they do it again. by the
third false alarm, an `UPDATE` row on this role earns a skim where it once earned a stop.

⚠️ **that is the harm, and it is not the wasted minutes.** `case=3`'s acceptance check is *"a single
`UPDATE` on an `ehmpathy-demo-oidc` row is a **STOP**"* — a rule that works only on a provisioner who
still reads `UPDATE` as a signal. **a role that cries wolf here spends the attention another
critipath depends on.**

⇒ so the felt difference between this case's success and its failure is small in the moment and
grows over months: **a diff you can skim, or a diff you must audit.** the aws-side mechanics that
decide which one you get — the trust-policy rewrite, the principal round-trip — live in the `[tn]`
below, where they belong as the *why* behind each `then`.

## what the two forms each caught

- the **narrative** caught the *compounding* cost, which no `then` can hold: a spurious `UPDATE` does
  not merely waste two minutes — it **spends the provisioner's attention**, and `case=3` is the
  critipath that later needs that attention intact. a behavior-level read sees an idempotency check;
  only the lived walk sees one case erode another.
- the **`[tn]`** caught `[t2]`, which the narrative could only gesture at. once cast as a `then`, it
  forced the honest admission that the expected outcome is **unverified** — and turned a comfortable
  assumption into a listed open question.

⚠️ **a narrative whose subject is the SYSTEM is a spec paragraph, never an experience.** one sentence
of lived encounter followed by three paragraphs on what `DeclaredAwsIamRole` compares, how aws
rewrites a deleted principal's arn, and whether the plan reads `UPDATE` or `KEEP` — that is a
restatement of a behavior.

⇒ **the tell is the grammatical subject.** count the sentences whose subject is a component rather
than a person — if the component wins, you have written a spec paragraph under a narrative heading.
the mechanics were not wrong and not deleted; they moved into `[t2]`, where a `^` note is exactly the
right shape for a *why-behind-a-then*.

🔴 **and the mis-home was easy to miss because the content was GOOD.** the arn-rewrite explanation
earns its place in this case — so the paragraph read as valuable, and value is not the test. **the
test is whose point of view carries it.**
