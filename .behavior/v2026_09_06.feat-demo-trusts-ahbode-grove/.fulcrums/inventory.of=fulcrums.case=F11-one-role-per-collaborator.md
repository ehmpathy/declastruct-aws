# F11 — one role per collaborator, never one shared

**rework** · clean ⏳ (shares F2's clock) · **status** · open · **confidence** · 90%

## 🔴 a FOURTH kind of row — a fork the WISHER opened mid-drive

F1–F8 record forks **met in the artifact and decided**. F9 records an axis **never walked**. F10
records a **rule invented mid-round**. this one is none of those: the wisher **widened the scope**
after the vision was written, and the widen created a fork that did not exist at write time.

⇒ **a scope widen is a fulcrum generator**, and its fork is the one a driver is least prepared for —
every extant artifact was priced against the narrower scope.

## the fork, stated fairly

the wish names **one** consumer: the ahbode camp grove. the wisher then widened the frame — *"the
ahbode camp account (or any third party collaborator account)"* and *"we want to give these
collaborator accounts access to demo."* ⇒ **the target must serve N collaborators, and every artifact
here priced exactly one.**

two shapes:

| shape | what it costs |
|---|---|
| **① one role per collaborator** — `ehmpathy-demo-for-grove`, `ehmpathy-demo-for-$x` | N roles, N trust policies, N attachments. no factory here to fold them (awkward 2) |
| **② one shared role, N principals** — `ehmpathy-demo-for-collaborators` | one role. but the name stops to name its consumer, cloudtrail cannot tell one org from another, and a revoke or a widen for one reaches all |

## taken, and why at the time

**① one role per collaborator.**

🔴 **the wish's own hard constraint decides it — one layer up from where the wish stated it.** R1 says
a reach's target is *"a role DEDICATED to this reach … never one shared with cicd — so a widen for
one consumer can never silently widen the other."* a shared collaborator role is **that same defect
between two collaborators** rather than between a grove and cicd. ⇒ the constraint's **letter** names
cicd; its **reason** covers this case exactly.

and the precedent is written, in the tree the wisher named: ahbode declares `ahbode-prep-for-grove`
**and** `ahbode-prod-for-grove` — **one role per (target account × consumer)** — never one
`ahbode-for-grove` that carries two accounts' worth of trust. `ehmpathy-demo-for-$x` is that family
with our org and account substituted, and F2's `-for-` slot already carries its rationale.

## 🔴 what ② would cost — three of this vision's own claims

| the claim | what ② does to it |
|---|---|
| **attribution** (pros, `case=5` `[t5]`) | it already cannot tell one **process** from another on a box. ② additionally cannot tell one **org** from another ⇒ the dedicated-role attribution win is spent |
| **revocation by declaration** (the aha) | a revoke for one collaborator becomes an **upsert of a document the others depend on**, so a slip cuts a party nobody meant to cut. under ① it edits a file only that collaborator's reach reads |
| **the R1 isolation** (F1's whole argument) | F1 already records the isolation as absent at two layers — the shared bundle, the runtime capability. ② adds a **third**: the trust policy itself, which is the one layer ① actually holds |

⚠️ **so ② does not merely add a risk. it spends the three wins a dedicated role was bought for**, and
one of them is this vision's headline.

## 🔴 the pick FOLDS a fifth dimension — and ② would not

`1.vision.experience.dimensions.md` records four latent dimensions, each folded with its own re-walk
trigger. **collaborator identity is a fifth**, and its fold is a **consequence of this pick** rather
than a fact about the domain:

| under | does it fold? | why |
|---|---|---|
| **①** | ✅ yes | each collaborator is a **replica** of the whole grid — same four actors, same three stages, same four reach-states, and not one cell's verdict moves. orthogonal and non-informative, which is what a fold is |
| **②** | ⛔ no | a revoke in one collaborator's row **changes another's cell**, so the axis carries information and the product must be walked |

⇒ **the re-walk trigger: a collaborator whose trust SHAPE differs** — a `Condition` (F9), a different
bundle (F1), or a different tier. a second collaborator that is a plain copy needs no walk.

## why the confidence is 90%

the precedent is written and cited, the wisher named it, and the wish's own R1 reason covers the case.

the 10% is **maintenance under N**: each collaborator is three declared resources with no factory to
fold them, and this repo holds **zero** `provision/**/*.test.ts` to catch a copy defect. that pressure
grows with N and is a real argument for a local helper — a decision for the day a **third**
collaborator arrives, never today (`rule.prefer.wet-over-dry`).

⚠️ **and the wisher named the precedent, never the fork.** they may not have seen ② as an option, so
this is confirmation of a shape rather than a verdict on a choice. that is why it reads 90% and not
higher.

## rework, and why it is clean

it shares **F2's clock**: a name is one line here until the paired ahbode tree merges, after which it
is a coordinated two-repo change. the pick itself is clean today — no caller has hardened against it,
and the second collaborator does not exist yet.

⚠️ **and it hardens in ONE direction only.** to add a second dedicated role later is cheap. to
**split** a shared role after two collaborators depend on it is a coordinated change in three trees,
with a dead reach in each.
