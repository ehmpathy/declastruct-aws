# inventory.of=fulcrums

## .what

the forks met on this drive that were **best-guessed and driven past**, per
`rule.always.defer-fulcrums-to-last` + `rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess`. each row
has an entry file beside this one. none halted the road.

**stages** · `1.vision` (F1–F11) · `5.1.execution.from_vision` (F12–F14) · `5.3.verification` (F15) ·
**as of** 2026-09-16 · **F1 ruled** · the rest open

⚠️ **one inventory per ROUTE, never per stage.** the execution stage's yield cites F2 and F11 from
this table rather than mint a second one, so a fulcrum found downstream lands here with its stage
named. ⇒ **a per-stage inventory would let one drive hold two registers of one concept**, which is
the split `rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms` forbids.

## the axes

- **rework** — `clean` (a rename, a swapped default, a re-scoped boundary that does not ripple) vs
  `dirty` (callers hardened against it, so reversal is a teardown). the council sorts on this.
- **confidence** — a percentage, with a reason. a call under 93% earns a row even where no
  alternative was weighed (`rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess`).

🔴🔴 **⚠️ `rework` measures reversibility of the ARTIFACT, never of the EXPOSURE — and on this wish
the two come apart.** the rule defines the axis purely in code terms (*"a rename, a swapped default,
a re-scoped boundary that does not ripple"*), which is right for a fulcrum over a name or a file
home. it **misreads** a fulcrum over a **permission or a trust**, where the code fix is one swapped
value and the consequence is not undoable at all:

| fulcrum | the code rework | 🔴 the exposure, if the pick is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** — the permission bound | one `document:` value | any credential issued in the window was **live**; `case=7` `[t5]` records that a planted role **survives the revoke** |
| **F9** — no `Condition` | append a `condition` field | same window, same residue |
| **F7** — the denies do not transfer | add statements to a bundle | same |
| F2 · F3 · F4 · F5 · F6 · F10 · F11 | a name, a path, a shape, a rule, a count | ✅ genuinely reversible — no live consequence at all |
| 🔴 **F15** — the permadiff fold | nine lines in one cast | ✅ reversible, and **a wrong pick fails LOUD** — the suite goes red, so no window opens at all |

⇒ **for the top three, `clean` is true of the diff and false of the world.** the vision states this
itself at `case=7` `[t5]` — *"the door shuts; the planted role survives it"* — so the two artifacts
held opposite views of one pick.

⚠️ **the column stays `clean`**, since the rule defines it on the code and a private redefinition
would be a synonym. what changes is that **the council must not read `clean` as *"low stakes"***;
for a permission fulcrum it means *"cheap to change, expensive to have been wrong."*

🔴 **and F1's verdict proves the distinction was worth the words.** the wisher upheld the pick, so its
`clean` code rework was never spent — but the **exposure** column is exactly what the verdict accepts:
a live credential, and a planted role that survives a revoke. ⇒ **a ruled permission fulcrum converts
its exposure row from a risk into a stated cost**, and the row must stay legible for that reason.

🔴 **F12's kind is the one most likely to go unrecorded, and its lesson is the sharpest.** the driver
did not weigh two branches and pick; the wish's acceptance list (*"unchanged — a diff proves it"*)
removed the branch that closes the gap, and the driver complied. ⇒ **compliance with a stated bound
still produces a residual, and only a fulcrum tells the council what their bound cost.** the tell is
not *"did I choose?"* but *"is there a fix I would have taken, and did a bound stop me?"*

**F10's trigger is the second one in `rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess`** — *"an
invented taxonomy or axis set"* and *"a call under 93% confidence… even where you weighed no
alternative."*

⚠️ **and it is the trigger a driver is least likely to feel.** a fork announces itself — two options
sit side by side. an invented rule feels like a **discovery**: it resolves a defect, it is applied
immediately, and it reads as found rather than chosen. ⇒ **the cue is not "did I choose?" but "would
I defend this at 93%?"** — and a rule authored and applied within one hunt almost never clears that.

🔴 **F9's axis is `Condition`.** a trust statement restricts on `Principal` **and** on `Condition`,
and only the first was walked. the primitive is expressible today
(`DeclaredAwsIamPolicyStatement.condition`, with unit coverage), and this vision even reaches for a
`Condition` as a **revoke** shape at `case=7` `[t3'']` — so the omission is neither a scope bound nor
an unavailable feature. ⇒ **a fulcrum inventory records the forks a driver saw; the axis a driver
never looked down leaves no trace at all**, which is why a targeted hunt is what finds it.

🔴 **F11's trigger is the one no self-review can reach, and that is its whole lesson.** the other
three kinds are found by a driver who looks harder at their own artifact. a **scope widen** is a fact
about the wish, held by the wisher, and it can invalidate a premise every artifact here shares. ⇒
**when the wisher speaks mid-drive, the ask is not only "does this change my answer?" but "does it
change what my artifacts were PRICED against?"** — F11 changed the second while the first held.

🔴 **F15 sits in kind 1 and carries a lesson on a DIFFERENT axis, so it earns no eighth row.** the
taxonomy above grades **how a fork reached the inventory**; F15 arrived as an ordinary fork. what is
new is how one branch **left** it:

the suite can run.

🔴 **three rows are owed a verdict, and they are owed in a fixed order — F2 → F8 → F1.**

⚠️ **the three are NAMED and never counted against a total, and that is deliberate.** a fraction over

⇒ **the merge is not one deadline in a queue — it is the pivot the queue sorts around.** the order
F2 → F8 → F1 is right, and its stated reason (the clock) is not why. ⚠️ **a right answer with a wrong
reason survives only until the inputs move**, which is exactly what this correction block records.

⚠️ **so *"F1 is settled last because it has no clock"* was true and incomplete.** F1 was not merely
un-hurried — it was said to be **not settleable at the council at all** until four checks ran, each
of which needs demo-account access.

⇒ 🔴 **and that second claim was WRONG, which the verdict proved on 2026-09-08.** the wisher ruled
F1 in one line, with no check run: *"yeah this is fine; demo account needs full access."* **the four
checks would have priced the decision; they were never a prerequisite for it.**

⚠️ **the durable correction: a prerequisite is a claim about what the DECIDER needs, and the decider
owns that claim.** a driver may state what a check would buy. it may not declare the verdict
unavailable until the check runs — that converts advice into a gate on someone else's authority.
⇒ the honest cost of the error is **one queue, never one round**: the four rows were correctly found
informative, and only their status as *gates* was the driver's to relinquish.

⇒ **the general form still holds: a sort key is a claim that it is the ONLY constraint.** this table
sorted by clock and, by its silence, asserted no other edge existed. two of three had one.

⚠️ **F1 was the one that mattered most, and it was NOT the one to settle first.** it is the wish's own
named fulcrum, it is shared with the paired ahbode tree, and it decides `case=5`'s verdict — but a
fulcrum with no clock can wait for a fulcrum that has one. **weight sets the depth of the verdict;
the clock sets its order.** ⇒ 🔴 **and the wisher then ruled it before F2 and F8 anyway**, which is
the decider's prerogative and costs the queue nothing: F2's clock has still not started (question 5),
and F8 still waits on the merge.

🔴 **F8 is new at review round 4, and it is the lowest-confidence call on this list.** it exists
because the vision's **headline** assumed a capability this repo lacks: `DeclaredAwsIamRoleDao`
declares `set.delete = null`, so *"delete the trust statement"* — the aha, repeated in six files — is
not expressible. the class is settled (an `upsert` of the **trust policy**), the instance is
deliberately left open, and **every candidate shape carries a real defect**.

⚠️ **its confidence has NOT moved, and the reason is worth a line.** a later hunt found the delete
surface is **split** — three iam DAOs *do* implement `set.delete` — which adds a fifth candidate (⑤,
strip the role's attachments) that is a genuine declarative delete. that **widens the options and
settles no pick**, and it brings a second unverified aws behaviour of its own. ⇒ more paths, same
uncertainty. see the entry.

⏳ **a rework can be clean only for a window, and TWO of these are.** F2's cleanliness expires when
the paired ahbode tree merges — after that the role name lives in another org's `sts:AssumeRole`
resource arn, so a rename is a coordinated two-repo change with a dead reach in between. F8's expires
at the first incident.

⚠️ **a flat `clean` in the rework column would have hidden both.** the column is what the council
sorts on, so a conditional rework must carry its condition **in the row**, never in the entry alone —
which is why F2 and F8 read `clean ⏳` rather than `clean`.

## the three that carry weight beyond a rename

- **F1** — a *security posture*, never a config choice. the vision states its cost plainly rather
  than assume it away (`1.vision.experience.case=5.*`), which is what makes it safe to drive past.
  ⬇⬇⬇⬇⬇ **72% → 65% → 58% → 52% → 44% → 38% across five review hunts.** the first two found a cost
  unpriced at write; the third found the fork had **not enumerated its own branches**; the fourth
  found it had **invented** the cost it declined them on; the fifth found the paired repo holds a
  **stated invariant the pick breaks**:
  - **65%** — reuse partly defeats the wish's own hard R1 constraint, whose stated reason is *"a
    widen for one consumer can never silently widen the other"*. a dedicated role isolates four
    layers and **not** the shared bundle.
  - 🔴 **58%** — and **not the runtime capability either.** the bundle carries
    `iam:UpdateAssumeRolePolicy` on `'*'`, so the credential this grant issues can rewrite
    `ehmpathy-demo-oidc`'s own trust policy (`case=5` `[t2']`). the isolation R1 exists to buy is
    not merely weakened at that layer — it is **inverted**.
  - 🔴 **52%** — and the fork was stated as a **binary** when it has **three** branches. the third —
    a per-target bundle **function** that spreads the shared statements and appends the target's own
    — is the **paved shape in the paired repo**, at two call sites, with a rationale that cites this
    same R1 constraint. it was declined here on two premises a census falsifies.
  - 🔴🔴 **44%** — and the cost it declined those branches on was **invented**. F1 priced them at
    *"one extra declaration, empty at first"*; **F5 already commits to that declaration**, and it is
    never empty. ⇒ the alternatives cost **zero new declared resources** — plus one expression, or
    one small function if ahbode's shape is mirrored.
  - 🔴🔴 **38%** — and the paired repo holds a **stated invariant this pick breaks**:
    *"EVERY `iam:` write needs the admin tier"* + *"the admin tier is HUMAN-ONLY in practice … which
    a clone cannot perform"* (`term=tier._.choice.reason.md:163,166-168`). ⇒ the earlier rows graded
    the tier word as a poor **fit**; this one finds the grantee's own repo declares that the identity
    which writes `iam:` is precisely the one a clone may never hold.

  the call stands on its empirical half — and now on that alone. the fork it hands the wisher has
  moved five times: from *purpose vs a foreign tier word*, to *purpose vs a stated wish constraint*,
  to *purpose vs the incumbent's own integrity*, to *a fork whose branch list was incomplete*, to *a
  fork whose cost column favoured the pick with a cost that does not exist* — and now to **a pick
  that breaks a declared invariant of the repo it serves**.

  ⚠️ **the last two moves are a different kind, and the fourth is the one to read.** the first three
  each raised the price of the pick. the fourth **lowered the price of every alternative**, and it
  did so by retraction of a cost this driver had supplied. ⇒ a confidence over a fork is a claim
  about its **branches** and their **prices**, not merely about the choice — and a self-supplied
  price that leans one way is the failure a fulcrum record exists to catch.
- **F7** — a deliberate divergence from the paired repo's precedent. anyone who reads both trees will
  see the mismatch and should find the reason recorded, never inferred.
  ⬆ **70% → 74%.** later the same round, a grep of `src/` split the fourth branch in two and showed
  it buys **less** than the falsification implied: the suite drives `AWS-RunShellScript` itself
  (`execCommand.ts:45`), so no document scope can put the shell *"out of reach by construction"* here
  as it does in ahbode prod. the branch narrows path (c) rather than a close of it. ⚠️ **the rise
  vindicates no part of the round-4 argument** — the decline's stated reason was still wrong; what
  changed is that the alternative it declined is weaker in **our** context than in the one it was
  quoted from. ⇒ a shape proven next door is proven **against their workload**, never ours.
  ⬇⬇ **91% → 78% → 70%.** at round 5 the fourth branch's *"unmeasured"* decline was falsified — the
  resource-scoped shape is **shipped in ahbode prod**, which grades the action-scoped shape this
  fulcrum took as a *"symptom patch"* — and the root-shell premise F7 cites is marked *"inferred, NOT
  measured"* at its own source while `case=5` states it flatly.
  ⬇ **91% → 78%.** its empirical half is untouched — the suite really does drive every action ahbode
  denies. but its three branches were **all scoped by ACTION**, and the fourth — scoped by
  **RESOURCE** — is the only one that narrows the surface without a suite break. ⚠️ **it is the same
  defect as F1's, on a different fulcrum, found by the same hunt** — a branch list assumed complete
  because the driver wrote it, where the paired repo held the branch one screen from a quote the
  fulcrum already used.
- **F8** — the *reversibility* of the whole grant, which is the vision's stated value proposition.
  a fulcrum where **every** branch carries a defect, so the honest output is priced candidates plus
  a raised find, never a confident pick.
