## .narrative

a reader of the diff asks: does `sts:TagSession` + `sts:SetSourceIdentity` open the role to anyone new? no. both are permissions-only actions that ride on the same `AssumeRole` call, gated by the same one `aws` principal. a foreign principal still meets the principal check first, and fails there.

the fail-safe has two layers, each fast and loud:
- every commit: `resources.reach.test.ts` [t0] pins exactly one statement, exactly one principal, no condition. a refactor that splits the actions into a second statement, or adds a principal, goes red before merge
- every apply: case=2's plan read shows the principal unchanged. a principal diff is a STOP
